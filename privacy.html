Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

The GTAclipz Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by GTAclipz to post its own videos to its own accounts.

What we collect

The app does not collect, store or share personal data of any other person. It only uses the sign-in tokens of GTAclipz's own accounts, which are kept on GTAclipz's own computer and used only to upload our videos and read basic information about our own posts.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Deletion

Tokens can be removed at any time by revoking the app's access in each platform's settings. To ask about this policy, email us.
