Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

The GTAclipz Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by GTAclipz to post its own videos to its own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X.

What we collect

The app does not collect, store or share personal data of any other person. It only uses the sign-in tokens of GTAclipz's own accounts, which are kept on GTAclipz's own computer and used only to upload our videos and read basic information about our own account and posts.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload GTAclipz's own videos to GTAclipz's own YouTube channel and to set their titles, descriptions, privacy status and thumbnails. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Through YouTube API Services the app only accesses the authorized channel's own information needed to upload videos (the OAuth access token and the IDs of the videos it uploads). It does not collect, store or share any YouTube data about other users, and it does not show YouTube content anywhere.

Storage and deletion

Sign-in tokens are stored only on GTAclipz's own computer. They are deleted when access is revoked or the app is no longer used. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (for YouTube) and in each platform's app settings. Any stored data is deleted within 30 days of revocation or on request by email.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
